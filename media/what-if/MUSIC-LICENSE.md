# Soundtrack Provenance

Current track: **Take this Higher**, by **Michael Ramir C.**, from
[Mixkit's euphoric music catalog](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/mood/euphoric/).

The [Mixkit Stock Music Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree)
was checked on 2026-09-03. It permits commercial web and social-media video,
including online advertisements. Do not reuse this music for TV/radio broadcasts,
video games, CDs/DVDs, a music-only release, or rights-management registration.
Do not claim ownership of the recording. Review the linked terms for new uses.

The edited excerpt is synchronized into the HunterAgent film, not distributed
as a standalone track. Source audio and the intermediate WAV stay out of git and
the source archive. `music.json` records the download URL, checksum and edit
settings so the film can be rebuilt. Attribution is retained here even though
the license does not require an on-screen credit.

The previous original piano/strings experiment is superseded; `score.swift`
is retained only as an earlier composition source, not used by current exports.
